BUHAY-AHBuildings-and-Bridges Unit-cost Hybrid Analytics and Yardstick for Accountable and Honest infrastructure

No blueprint yet? Tell BUHAY-AH what the school, health station, evacuation center or bridge must hold, or how much money you have. It designs the facility, takes off every quantity under NSCP, DPWH and AASHTO rules, and gives you a lean start quotation, a fair estimate and a ceiling. Give it a contract amount and it tells you whether the project is over-budgeted.

How it works

From a need or a budget to a fair price

01

Start anywhere

A requirement (classrooms, beds, staff, evacuees, stalls), a budget, a DXF floor plan, or one of 80 design templates (schools, hospitals, terminals, bridges, runways, piers).

02

Design

Reusable space modules on DepEd, DOH, NBC and DSWD norms are laid out into a schematic plan you can edit, save and combine.

03

Quotation

Quantities from NSCP 2015, DPWH Blue Book and AASHTO rules, priced with DPWH pay items into lean, standard and ceiling ABCs.

04

Over-budget check

The over-budget ratio, an XGBoost cost model and an Isolation Forest combine into the BUHAY-AH Risk Index. SHAP explains every result.

The research

BUHAY-AH: A budget-to-blueprint design generator, standards-based quantity take-off and hybrid machine-learning system for start quotations and over-budget detection in public buildings, hospitals, transport terminals, bridges, airports and seaports

BUHAY-AH turns a requirement or a budget into a schematic design, prices it with a standards-based quantity take-off, and screens contract amounts with Multiple Linear Regression, XGBoost, Isolation Forest and SHAP. On a 300-project simulated benchmark with known overpricing, the standard estimate came within 5.2% (buildings) and 7.7% (bridges) of fair awarded costs, and the BUHAY-AH Risk Index caught 94.4% of padded contracts (AUC 0.987) where an Isolation Forest alone caught 27.8%.

BUHAY-AH is a screening tool. A flag means a contract deserves a closer look by the implementing office, the Commission on Audit, the Office of the Ombudsman or the Independent Commission for Infrastructure. It is not proof of irregularity.

Unit costs are indicative 2025 Bicol rates and can be edited in the app. Designs are schematic and must be completed by licensed architects and engineers before construction.
