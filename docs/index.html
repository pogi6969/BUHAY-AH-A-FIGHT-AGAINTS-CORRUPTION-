How it works

From plan to fair price in four steps

01

Provide the plan

Start from a reference plan, import a DXF floor plan, or type the dimensions: storeys, spans, widths, foundation.

02

Quantity take-off

Concrete, rebar, formwork, masonry, roofing, piles and girders are computed with NSCP 2015, DPWH Blue Book and AASHTO rules.

03

Quotation

DPWH pay items and Bicol unit costs build the ABC with D.O. 197 s. 2016 / D.O. 30 s. 2025 markups, in lean, standard and ceiling tiers.

04

Over-budget check

The over-budget ratio, an XGBoost cost model, and an Isolation Forest combine into the BUHAY Risk Index. SHAP explains every result.

The research

BUHAY: A blueprint-driven quantity take-off and hybrid machine-learning system for standards-based start quotations and over-budget detection in public buildings and bridges

The study builds a standards-based quantity take-off engine for public buildings and bridges in Bicol Region V and couples it with Multiple Linear Regression, XGBoost, Isolation Forest and SHAP. On a 300-project simulated benchmark with known overpricing, the standard estimate came within 5.2% (buildings) and 7.7% (bridges) mean absolute percentage error of fair awarded costs, and the BUHAY Risk Index caught 94.4% of padded contracts (AUC 0.987) where an Isolation Forest alone caught 27.8%.
